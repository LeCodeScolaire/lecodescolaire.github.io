Le Code Scolaire is an educational channel that publishes short animated science lessons (physics, chemistry, mathematics, biology) for middle and high school students.

The Le Code Scolaire Publisher app is an internal tool used only by the channel owner to upload and schedule the channel's own videos to its own TikTok account.
