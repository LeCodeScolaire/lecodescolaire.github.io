1. Who we are

Le Code Scolaire publishes educational videos. This policy explains how the Le Code Scolaire Publisher app (the "App") handles data.

2. Data we access

When the account owner connects the App to TikTok, the App receives, through TikTok's official authorization (OAuth):

  • basic profile information of the owner's own TikTok account (account identifier, display name, avatar);
  • an access token allowing the App to upload and publish videos to that same account.

The App does not access, collect or store any data about other TikTok users, viewers, followers or commenters.

3. How we use it

This data is used only to upload, schedule and publish Le Code Scolaire's own videos and to show the owner the status of these uploads.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely and only on the owner's own devices or the publishing service used by the owner. They are never sold, shared or used for advertising.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties. Video files and their descriptions are sent only to TikTok, in order to publish them.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only as long as the TikTok connection is active. The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions); stored tokens are then deleted. Deletion requests can be sent to the contact address below.

7. Children

Our videos are educational content for students, but the App itself is not used by children and does not collect data from anyone other than the account owner.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date at the top shows the latest version.
